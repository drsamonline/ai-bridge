## 3. What it does on your machine

- It connects to a **Chrome DevTools Protocol endpoint on localhost** to drive a tab you opened, or a browser it launches with a profile of its own when you set `browser.mode: "launch"`.
- It reads the page it is driving, and it writes to your workspace **only** through edits you accept, hunk by hunk.
- It keeps project state locally, so a conversation can survive a new chat.

Point it at a browser profile you are willing to have automated. The dedicated launch profile exists so that your everyday browsing is not the one being driven.

What you type does travel: your prompt goes to whichever provider you are driving, under **their** privacy terms and retention rules. If a prompt contains code you cannot disclose, do not send it.
